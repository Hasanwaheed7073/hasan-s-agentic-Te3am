Import cookies

  1. In your normal Chrome (where you're already signed in), install a cookie export extension such as Cookie-Editor.
  2. Open the site, e.g. linkedin.com, click the extension, then Export → JSON. It copies to your clipboard.
  3. Paste it below, or choose an exported file, then click Import.

🔒 Cookies work like passwords. They go only into the team browser on this PC and are never shown to the AI. Don't share the export file, and delete it after importing.
